Our Mission & History

Established in 2005, The 7 Best Private Schools in Houston has a rich history of providing a high-quality education to families in the Houston area. Our school is committed to academic excellence, character development, and community engagement.

Our teaching approach is centered around the idea that every child learns differently. We use a combination of traditional and innovative methods to engage our students and help them achieve their full potential.

Beyond the classroom, our students have many opportunities to develop their passions and interests. From art and music to sports and community service, we offer a wide range of extracurricular activities that help our students grow into well-rounded individuals.
